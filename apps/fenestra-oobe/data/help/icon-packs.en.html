Bring your own icons

Fenestra doesn't include or download icons from other companies. You can export the ones from your own PC and use them here.

  1. On your Windows PC, download and run Fenestra Icon Extractor from the Fenestra releases page. It saves your icons, cursors and fonts in a single .zip file.
  2. Upload that .zip anywhere you like, for example your cloud drive, and copy a share or download link.
  3. Paste the link in the address bar above and press Enter. When the download finishes, Fenestra checks the pack and shows you a preview before applying it.

Direct links ending in .zip are downloaded straight away. Downloads are saved in your Downloads folder. Icon packs are installed only for your account.
